 DESCRIPTION (paste as-is)
========================================================================

Always Scan keeps the game's own scanner highlight active at all times,
without holding the handheld scanner, and expands it from the vanilla
center circle to a configurable radius around the player.

The visuals are the vanilla feature: the plugin drives the game's own
outline system, so colors, thickness and pulse are identical to what the
handheld scanner draws.

Features:
- Always on: highlighting stays visible while you run, fight, loot or fly.
- Full radius: objects inside the configured radius (default 50 m) are
  outlined in every direction, not just the center circle.
- Category colors: lootable items (blue), containers (orange), bodies
  and corpses - humans, creatures, wrecked robots and turrets (orange),
  interactive devices and terminals (green), doors (red), flora (green).
- Bodies behave like containers: loot them empty and the outline goes
  away. Anything you cannot take does not count as loot.
- Toggle hotkey (default F8) with a short HUD message.
- Interaction safe: activation, pick-up, dialogue, doors, computers and
  crafting all behave exactly like vanilla.

IMPORTANT - RUNTIME REQUIREMENT:
This mod is an SFSE (Starfield Script Extender) plugin. Its highlighting
logic is a compiled DLL that calls the game's internal outline system,
which is not exposed to Papyrus scripts. This submission therefore ships
as the ESM plugin and its Papyrus helper only, and WILL NOT light
anything up without the SFSE plugin DLL on the player's machine.
The DLL cannot be included here because Creations do not permit external
code / script-extender dependencies, and consoles cannot load DLLs.
We are submitting this package to ask whether an SFSE-dependent build
can be distributed through Creations at all; if the answer is no,
that is understood and this submission can be discarded.
